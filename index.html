About us

We are a Ladies Homestay for college girls and working women. The owners family lives in the downstairs. The family provides home cooked food to guests. The family acts as a local guardian and supports them with needs like shopping or medical help.

We are located opposite to My G-future, a walk-able distance from Christ College, St. Joseph's College and conveniently located on Trissur Road, Tana, Irinjalakuda.
